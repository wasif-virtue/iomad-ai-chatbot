
# Moodle Frequently Asked Questions

## How do learners access a course?
Learners generally sign in to their Moodle site and open a course they are enrolled in. The exact steps depend on the site's configuration.

## How do teachers create a quiz?
Teachers with the required permissions can use Moodle's course editing tools to add a Quiz activity, configure its settings, add questions, and save the activity.

## What is the difference between a resource and an activity?
A resource provides learning material, such as a file or page. An activity usually asks learners to participate or complete a task, such as a quiz, assignment, or forum discussion.
