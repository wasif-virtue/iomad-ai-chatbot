
# Moodle Basics

## What is Moodle?
Moodle is a free, open-source learning management system (LMS). Educational institutions and organizations use it to create online courses, share learning resources, conduct assessments, and support online learning.

## Courses
A Moodle course is an online learning space. Teachers can organize learning materials into sections or topics and provide resources and activities to learners.

## Activities and resources
Activities can include assignments, quizzes, forums, and other interactive learning tasks. Resources can include files, pages, URLs, and other learning materials.

## Users and roles
Moodle supports different user roles and permissions. What a person can do depends on their assigned role and the permissions configured for the site.
